    How the value works
    
Each side gives the players it picks and gets the other side's picks.
    ROS is rest-of-season z: per-game z in 9 categories times games left, the same as season_plan.py.
    The winner is the team with the larger gain in its best-10 total, with equal category weights. Gains closer than the even band count as an even trade.
    "Your wt" uses the category weights in cat_weights.json. PV is perceived value, used for the "would accept" check.
    The check runs the Python model (trade_eval.py) in this browser with Pyodide, on the data of the last sync. The first check loads Pyodide and numpy (about 10 MB, then cached).

      How this works
      
Each cell is the chance you win a one-week matchup (more categories won than lost), from the same simulation as the Sync tab. Select a team to see the category chances.
      Strength: rosters only. Every player healthy, and every NBA team plays 4 games on the same 4 days. This removes injuries and schedule.
      Next week: the next full matchup week with injuries as they are now (an OUT player comes back after 7 days), the same week for every team.
      When you play: your real week against that team. The current matchup uses the live score. Weeks where ESPN has not set the NBA games yet (NBA Cup) show the Strength result.
      Moves after today (streams, trades, waivers) are not included for either side. Rank and value come from the last sync (sum of the top 10 rest-of-season z).
